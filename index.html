Understand First

What They Are Really Asking

It is not a biography. It is a relevance check.

Can you speak clearly?

  • Short, structured sentences

Are you relevant?

  • Skills match this role

Is your story consistent?

  • Resume and words agree

Will you fit the team?

  • Attitude and energy
Golden rule: everything you say must answer one question — “why should we hire you as a fresher?”

The Formula

Past → Present → Future

Most of the time goes to what you have done. Very little to dreams.

The Ideal Answer

A Ready Script — Say It Your Way

Hi, I'm [Your Name], a final-year Computer Science student at [College]. I've built [Project Name] using [Tech], which [result in one line]. I also interned at [Company], where I [action + outcome]. I'm comfortable with [3 skills], and I'm looking for a role where I can grow as a [target role] and contribute from day one.
Name Education Proof of work Skills Goal
Time it: 60–90 seconds. If it runs past 2 minutes, cut the family and hobby parts.
Block 1

Name & Basics

DO

  • “I'm Rahul Sharma”
  • Add city in one line
  • One line role: “final-year CS student”
  • Smile before you speak

DON'T

  • ❌ “Myself Rahul”
  • ❌ Full home address
  • ❌ Nicknames or pet names
  • ❌ Age unless asked
Speak it: “Hi, I'm Rahul Sharma from Pune — a final-year Computer Science student.” That's it. Move on.
Block 1b

Family Background

One sentence maximum. It is context, not the story.

DO

  • One line: “My father runs a business, my mother is a teacher.”
  • Mention if it shaped your work ethic
  • Keep it positive and short

DON'T

  • ❌ Relatives' jobs and family history
  • ❌ Personal or financial problems
  • ❌ More than 2 sentences
Shortcut: if your family background isn't relevant, skip it. Nobody marks you down for leaving it out.
Block 2

Education Background

DO

  • Degree + college + year
  • CGPA only if 7.5+
  • Name 2 relevant subjects
  • Mention a final-year project if strong

DON'T

  • ❌ School marks and 10th/12th details
  • ❌ Every subject you studied
  • ❌ Backlogs unless asked
Example: “I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science from XYZ College in 2025, with a CGPA of 8.2.”
Block 3

Internship

Company + role + one outcome. Nothing else.

The 4-Part Line

At [Company] as a [role] for [duration], I [action + result].
  • ✅ “At Infosys as a backend intern for 3 months, I built 6 REST APIs used by an internal dashboard.”

DON'T

  • ❌ List daily tasks
  • ❌ Complain about the company
  • ❌ Say “I just did training”
  • ❌ Claim work you didn't do
Block 4

Projects

Two projects, said well, beat eight said badly.

One-Line Formula

Built [what] using [tech] that [result].
  • ✅ “Built a React expense tracker with Node and MongoDB, now used by 50+ students.”
  • Mention the live link or GitHub

DON'T

  • ❌ Explain the full architecture
  • ❌ Name tutorial clone projects only
  • ❌ List 8 projects
Block 4b

Any Real Experience Counts

Fresher does not mean zero experience.

Freelance Work

  • Client name (if allowed)
  • What you delivered

College Tech Team

  • Club or fest role
  • Team size handled

Teaching / Tutoring

  • Shows communication
  • Great for soft skills
Never fake experience. One honest “I built this myself” beats an invented internship.
Block 5

Skills & Certifications

DO

  • 3–4 skills from the job description
  • One language + one tool + one cloud
  • One certification, maximum
  • Say what you can build, not just “know”

DON'T

  • ❌ “I know everything”
  • ❌ 20 skills in one breath
  • ❌ MS Word and typing speed
  • ❌ Skills you can't answer questions on
Test: if they ask “show me one thing you built with it”, every listed skill must pass.
Block 6

Achievements

Numbers make people remember you.

What to Pick

  • Hackathon rank or participation
  • Scholarship or academic rank
  • Open-source contributions
  • Published paper or blog
  • Competition with a number attached

DON'T

  • ❌ School drawing prizes
  • ❌ Attendance certificates
  • ❌ Anything you can't explain
Block 6b

Hobbies & Extras

Keep 1–2 that show a trait: discipline, teamwork or creativity.

Shows Discipline

  • Sport, gym, running

Shows Creativity

  • Blogging, design, music

Shows Teamwork

  • Clubs, volunteering
Safe answer: “I enjoy badminton and I write short tech blogs.” Two lines. Done.
Block 6c

Career Goal — The Closing Line

DO

  • “In 3 years I want to be a strong backend engineer.”
  • Tie it to this role and this company
  • End with confidence, not apology

DON'T

  • ❌ “I want to go abroad”
  • ❌ “I want to start my own company”
  • ❌ “I want to learn everything”
  • ❌ “I'll do anything you give me”
Checklist

8 Things You Must Do

1 · Keep it 60–90 seconds

2 · Follow the 6-block order

3 · Use numbers and results

4 · Mirror the job description

5 · Speak in “I”, not “we”

6 · Pause between blocks

7 · End with your goal

8 · Practice out loud, on camera

Checklist

8 Things That Kill Your Answer

1 · “Myself Rahul…”

2 · Repeating your resume

3 · Long family history

4 · “I'm a hardworking team player”

5 · Complaining about anyone

6 · Memorised, robotic tone

7 · Talking for 5 minutes

8 · Saying “that's all” at the end

Delivery

How You Say It Matters More

Eye Contact

  • Look at the speaker

Pace

  • Slower than you think

Smile

  • Especially at the start

Hands

  • Relaxed, not in pockets

Pause

  • Silence beats “umm”

Volume

  • Clear, not loud
Practice

7-Day Plan + Likely Follow-ups

One Week

  • Day 1–2: write the 6 blocks
  • Day 3–4: say it out loud, record
  • Day 5: cut it to 90 seconds
  • Day 6: say it to a friend
  • Day 7: final polish, no memorising

Be Ready For

  • “Why this company?”
  • “Tell me about that project.”
  • “What's your biggest weakness?”
  • “Where do you see yourself in 5 years?”
  • “Why should we hire you?”
